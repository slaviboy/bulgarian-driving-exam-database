# Turkish (tr) translation notes

Format: id — problem — decision.

## General decisions

- предимство — Turkish law separates right of way (geçiş hakkı, KTK Art. 57/74) from the special priority of emergency vehicles (geçiş üstünlüğü, KTK Art. 61). — предимство = geçiş hakkı; специален режим на движение = geçiş üstünlüğü.
- изпреварване / заобикаляне / разминаване — "geçme" alone is ambiguous in Turkish. — sollama / (duran araç veya engelin) yanından geçme / karşılaşma.
- товарен автомобил — "kamyon" in Turkish law means a goods vehicle over 3,5 t only. — yük taşıtı.
- габаритни светлини vs светлини за паркиране — two different Bulgarian lamps. — konum lambaları (the ECE/Turkish homologation term for position lamps) vs park lambaları.
- генератор vs алтернатор — the bank defines the alternator as a kind of generator (iaaa-7587). — jeneratör / alternatör.
- пътно платно vs платно за движение — the bank treats "пътно платно" as the wider road formation (iaaa-5230: it includes the shoulders, the carriageway and the islands; a shoulder "е част от пътното платно"). — пътно платно = "yol platformu" (Turkish highway term for carriageway + shoulders), платно за движение = "taşıt yolu", everywhere, including rule questions such as "най-вдясно на пътното платно".
- ППС / МПС / автомобил — Turkish law nests araç ⊃ motorlu araç (all motorised vehicles, incl. tractors and work machines) ⊃ motorlu taşıt (motor vehicles for carrying people or goods). — ППС = araç, МПС = motorlu araç, автомобил = otomobil in everyday use, but "motorlu taşıt" where the Bulgarian means the legal class that includes lorries and buses. Test case iaaa-5324…5329 ("Кое от показаните превозни средства е автомобил?"): the correct pictures are lorries/vans, the wrong ones a tractor, a tram and a loader; "otomobil" (= passenger car in Turkish) would make the lorries wrong, "motorlu taşıt" keeps every answer's status. Same for "автомобил от категория С" (iaaa-11840/11827) and a vehicle over 3500 kg (iaaa-11823). (Parts 01–05 were first written with МПС = motorlu taşıt and were re-mapped before part 06.)
- ватман — "vatman", the usual Turkish word for a tram driver; тягов електродвигател = "cer (elektrik) motoru" (Turkish rail term).
- контролни точки — Bulgarian points are deducted; Turkish "ceza puanı" adds up. — kontrol puanları.
- ЗДвП — not abbreviated, to avoid confusion with Turkey's KTK. — Karayolu Trafik Kanunu.
- Colon stems — Turkish is verb-final, so the stem is built as a subject/topic ("X:" + "… yol açar") or with a cataphoric "şu/şunun …:" so that every answer completes it. Number-marked cataphors (şunlar) are avoided where they could hint at the number of correct answers.

- мигащ vs проблясващ — both occur in iaaa-12075 ("жълта мигаща или проблясваща светлина"). — мигащ = "yanıp sönen" (as in yanıp sönen sarı ışık), проблясващ = "çakar" (beacon); iaaa-12073 follows the same split.
- преминавам покрай vs заобикалям — "yanından geçmek" is reserved for заобикаляне (legal term); generic "преминавам покрай X" is "X'in yakınından geçmek" (iaaa-5383, 5799, 6032).
- Picture labels — Latin labels stay Latin (A, B, C in iaaa-11575/11579/11580). In iaaa-5294…5296 the picture shows Cyrillic "а.", "б.", "в."; "а" = Latin "a" (identical), but "б" and "в" have no identical Latin letter (Latin "b" would be matched to "в", which looks like a small B), so they are kept as printed, with lint exceptions.
- бавнодвижещо се ППС — "yavaş giden araç".
- авариен сигнал / аварийни светлини — the everyday "dörtlü flaşör" ("four-way flasher") would give away the correct answer of iaaa-6060 ("all direction indicators at the same time"). — The legal term "tehlike uyarı işareti / tehlike uyarı ışıkları" is used everywhere (5472-a2 and 6065-a4 were re-mapped).
- стартер / пускова уредба — iaaa-7589: "marş" in the answers would echo the stem "Marş motoru" and make the two correct answers look tautological. — стартер = marş motoru, пускова уредба = çalıştırma sistemi, пусков електродвигател = çalıştırma elektrik motoru (7582-a2 follows).
- стоп-сигнал vs стоп-светлини — iaaa-7623: "fren lambası" would echo the wrong answer "fren sistemi" (спирачната уредба). — стоп-сигнал = "stop lambası", стоп-светлини = "fren lambaları".
- T17 coupling devices — the terms follow the Turkish version of the UN/ECE coupling rules: теглително-прикачно устройство = çeki bağlantı tertibatı, ухо = çeki gözü, ключалка = bağlantı başlığı, съединителен сферичен болт = bağlantı topu, седлово устройство = beşinci teker tertibatı, царски болт = bağlantı pimi (king pin), теглич = çeki oku, болт-ухо = pim-göz.
- работни / неработни дни — "tatil günleri" is the natural Turkish for days off (weekends and public holidays) (iaaa-6241).
- сляп (white cane, iaaa-6327) — "görme engelli", the term of the Turkish traffic rules; глухоням = "işitme ve konuşma engelli" in the same form.
- предупредителен знак vs авариен сигнал — both could be "tehlike uyarı işareti" in Turkish. — The warning road sign is "uyarı işareti"; "tehlike uyarı işareti / ışıkları" is kept only for the hazard warning signal of the lights.
- Sign and signal names — the quoted names are rendered with one Turkish wording each, the same in every question (“Geçiş yasaktır”, “Geçiş serbesttir”, “Dikkat”, “Dikkat, dur!”, “Durmadan geçiş yasaktır”, “Yol boşsa geç”, “Geçiş hakkı olan yolda gidenlere yol ver”, “Karşı yönden gelen araçlara yol ver”, “… girmesi yasaktır”); 5159-a2 was harmonised to "yol boşsa geç". Lane-control signals: Навлизането е разрешено/забранено = “Giriş serbesttir/yasaktır”.
- разрешено / забранено as one-word answers — "serbesttir / yasaktır" after a stem "X-mak:" (Bulgarian "Пресичането на … е:"), the same everywhere.
- принудително спиране vs задължително спиране — both would be "zorunlu durma" in Turkish. — място/площадка за принудително спиране = "acil durma yeri / cebi" (лента за принудително спиране stays "emniyet şeridi"); място за задължително спиране = "zorunlu durma yeri". In running text "принудително спрял" = "zorunlu olarak durmuş".
- движение назад vs завиване в обратна посока — "geri" would cover both. — движение назад = "geri manevra", завиване в обратна посока = "geri dönüş".
- винаги as a one-word answer — "daima" everywhere (6053-a3 harmonised).
- велосипедна пътека — in the parking rules it stands next to "пешеходна пътека" with the same "5 m before it" rule (iaaa-5630, ЗДвП art. 98), i.e. the marked cycle crossing. — "bisiklet geçidi", parallel to "yaya geçidi".
- автомобилен път — the bank uses it for a road class with its own rules (no mopeds, tractors or bicycles; no U-turn, no reversing, no parking outside marked places: iaaa-5391, 5411–5418, 11354) and as a sign name (5164, 11601, 11603). Turkish "karayolu" means any road (and is used for "road transport" in T14/T15), so it would turn these rules into rules for all roads. — "motorlu taşıt yolu" (literal "road for motor vehicles") everywhere; 6041-a3, 5164-a3, 11601-a3, 11603-a2, 5752-a4 and 11711 were re-mapped from "karayolu".
- Агенция „Пътна инфраструктура“ — "Karayolu" is kept out of names for the same reason as above. — “Yol Altyapısı” Ajansı (iaaa-7012, 7014, 7015, 7027); областно пътно управление = il yol idaresi.
- пострадал / пострадали хора — "yaralı / yaralılar" in all accident questions (T21, T29, T30); "kazada zarar gören kişiler" in the insurance question iaaa-7143.
- лишаване от право да управлява МПС vs губи правоспособността — the first is a penalty, the second the loss of the entitlement after all control points are deducted. — "motorlu araç kullanma hakkından yoksun bırakma" vs "motorlu araç sürme hakkını kaybetmek" (iaaa-5503); глоба = para cezası, проверочен изпит = kontrol sınavı.
- Bus classes — UNECE classes: клас I / II / III = "I. / II. / III. sınıf otobüs", клас А / В = "A / B sınıfı otobüs" (Latin letters); стоящи / седящи пътници = ayakta / oturan yolcular.
- контролни органи — "denetim organları" everywhere (органите за контрол на МВР = "İçişleri Bakanlığının denetim organları"); the earlier "denetim görevlileri" in parts 08, 10, 17 and 21 was harmonised in the final pass, and 6825-a3 / 6016-a1 (same Bulgarian answer) now read the same.
- „сгъване“ на състава (jackknifing) — "kombinasyonun “makaslaması”", the Turkish truckers' term, with the quotation marks of the source.

## Interface (ui-strings.json)

- ИААА — the full glossary name (“Otomobil İdaresi” Yürütme Ajansı) is too long for buttons and short notices. — Spelled out once, in the legal attribution ("“Otomobil İdaresi” Yürütme Ajansının (IAAA)"); everywhere else the Latin abbreviation "IAAA" (suffixes with an apostrophe: IAAA'nın).
- категория — "sınıf", as in the questions ("B sınıfı"); the tab and screen are "Sınıflar", "Категория %1$s" = "%1$s sınıfı", "Моята категория" = "Sınıfım".
- вариант (the candidate's case: first licence or already holding one) — "durum" ("Durumunuzu seçin", "%1$d durum"), matching the onboarding text "Изберете вашия случай".
- отметка / отбелязани (bookmarks) — "kaydetmek / Kaydedilenler" (the usual Turkish app wording for saved items); "отбелязан в зелено" (highlighted) stays "yeşille işaretlendi".
- издание (dated edition of the question bank) — "yayım" ("%1$s tarihli yayım"); версия = "sürüm"; инсталиране = "kurma / Kuruluyor…" so that it is not confused with "Yükleniyor…" (loading).
- Plurals — Turkish uses the singular after a number ("3 soru", "40 dakika"), so the forms "one" and "other" are identical; "X от Y" counts are written "X / Y" ("Öğrenilen: %1$d / %2$d soru", "Geçme puanı: %1$d / %2$d").
- Percent — Turkish puts the sign before the number: "%1$d%% верни отговора" = "%%%1$d doğru cevap" (shows "%85 doğru cevap").
- Captions under big numbers (feature_*_fact_*, feature_home_answered …) are number-neutral nouns ("soru", "puan", "ardışık gün", "başarılı test").
- Buttons use the Turkish Android imperative ("Kapat", "İptal", "Tekrar dene", "Kontrol et"); messages use the formal "siz" ("seçin", "dokunun"). The quoted button in feature_practice_multi_hint is exactly "Kontrol et".

## Open doubts

- ЗДвП = "Karayolu Trafik Kanunu" (not abbreviated, to keep it apart from Turkey's 2918 sayılı KTK); a reviewer may prefer another name.
- МПС = "motorlu araç" (Turkish legal nesting araç ⊃ motorlu araç ⊃ motorlu taşıt) where the pivot suggests "motor vehicle"; автомобил as a legal class = "motorlu taşıt".
- пътно платно = "yol platformu" (wider formation) vs платно за движение = "taşıt yolu"; the pair is kept apart everywhere, but "yol platformu" is a technical term that learners may not know.
- автомобилен път = "motorlu taşıt yolu" (no official Turkish name for the Vienna E,6a road class); скоростен път = "ekspres yol"; автомагистрала = "otoyol".
- велосипедна пътека = "bisiklet geçidi" (from the parking rule context); in other contexts it could mean a cycle lane.
- iaaa-12060-a3 — marked correct although it looks surprising for a tunnel (see Source problems).
- б / в picture labels (iaaa-5294…5296) kept in Cyrillic as printed, with lint exceptions.
- специализиран превоз = "özel düzenli taşıma"; ККМ = "temas-kablo şebekesi"; колесар = "dingil arabası"; УПК expanded as "mesleki yeterlilik belgesi".
- фиш / акт = "ceza fişi" / "tutanak": Turkish has no exact equivalent of the Bulgarian two-step penalty documents.
