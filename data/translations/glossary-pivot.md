# Pivot glossary — Bulgarian road-traffic terms

This glossary explains what the Bulgarian terms of the question bank **mean**. Definitions follow the Bulgarian
Road Traffic Act (ЗДвП, §6 of its additional provisions) and the EU texts. The English column is a *pivot* for
understanding. Each language translates the **Bulgarian concept** into its own official legal term (see
`style-guide.md` §3) and records the choice in its own `glossary.json`.

Inflected forms appear in the questions:
* the definite article is a suffix: водачът/водача = the driver, автомобилът/автомобила = the car;
* plural: водачите, автомобилите.
