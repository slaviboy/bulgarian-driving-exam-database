# Notes — Spanish (es-ES)

Open questions and decisions, by id. Format: **id** — problem → decision.

## General decisions

- **платно за движение / пътно платно** — the bank treats them as two different things (iaaa-11575, iaaa-11579,
  iaaa-11580 ask which part of the road is which; two questions of part 08 speak of "the other structural elements
  of the пътно платно"). → платно за движение = «calzada»; пътно платно = «plataforma» everywhere, so that the two never
  collapse into one Spanish word. In the picture of those questions «plataforma» is the full width (C).
- **правоспособност / свидетелство за управление** → «habilitación para conducir» (the entitlement) /
  «permiso de conducción» (the document), so that the two stay distinct.
- **населено място** → «poblado» (Spanish legal term: en poblado / fuera de poblado).
- **МПС** → «vehículo de motor»; as in Spanish law, it excludes mopeds (ciclomotores), which matches ЗДвП.
- **Typographical errors** in the Bulgarian that do not change the meaning (e.g. "хлъзгъв", "скоросттта",
  "арматулното", "мужду", "правволинейното") are translated as the intended word and not listed one by one.

## Correction #1 of the brief (2026-11-05) — road classes

- **автомагистрала / скоростен път / автомобилен път** → «autopista» / «autovía» / «vía para automóviles»: three
  distinct classes. "автомобилен път" is the signed road for motor vehicles (white car on blue, Vienna sign E,6a;
  closed to mopeds, tractors and bicycles; no U-turn or reversing; the general 90 km/h limit, iaaa-11711), for which
  Spanish law has the term «vía para automóviles». "скоростен път" is the expressway with separate carriageways
  (120 km/h for B) → «autovía», the Spanish class between «autopista» and ordinary roads.
- Before the correction I had «carretera» for "автомобилен път" and «vía para automóviles» for "скоростен път"
  (following the old pivot). Changed in the finished parts: 6041 (07); 5164, 11601, 11603 (08); 5752 (14); 11711 (15);
  5391, 5411, 5412, 5413, 5416, 5417, 5418, 11354 (16) → «vía para automóviles»; 11743 (05); 8280 (14); 11712 (15);
  8276, 8282 (16); 8274, 8277, 11351 (17) → «autovía». Parts 19, 20 and 24 use the new terms from the start. All touched
  parts re-linted to 0 errors.
- «carretera» now appears only in fixed expressions that do not translate these terms: «luces de carretera»,
  «asistencia en carretera», «transporte (internacional) por carretera», «servicio(s) de carreteras», «dirección
  provincial de carreteras», «autoridades de carreteras» and the law name «Ley de Tráfico por Carretera» (ЗДвП);
  checked over all 25 parts at the end.
- While doing this, the 14 speed-limit stems of part 15 ("Когато няма други ограничения, …") were harmonised with part
  05: «Cuando no hay otras restricciones, …» (part 15 had «Si no hay…»).
- Corrections #2–#4 needed no change: «plataforma» / «calzada» were already distinct everywhere; 5295/5296 keep the
  printed «б» / «в» with "cyrillic" lint exceptions. For 5176/5177/5178 ("моторни" as the plain adjective), review
  wave 2 replaced «de motor» (which is the МПС term and excludes mopeds in Spanish law) with the plain adjective
  «motorizados» (glossary «моторни (ППС)»).

## Part 07

- **iaaa-5294/5295/5296 (picture labels)** — the pictures show three signs marked „а.”, „б.”, „в.” in Cyrillic.
  „а” → Latin «a» (identical shape). For „б” there is no Latin look-alike, and „в” looks like a Latin B, so a
  transliteration (b, v) would send the learner to the wrong sign. → the printed letters «б» and «в» are kept as they
  appear in the picture (lint exceptions for the Cyrillic check). If the app must contain no Cyrillic at all, the
  alternative is a positional description; this needs a decision by the project.
- **МПС със специален режим на движение** — changed from «vehículo prioritario» to the literal «vehículo de motor con
  régimen especial de circulación» (also in part 06, iaaa-6061). Reasons: the bank says these vehicles "имат
  предимство" («tienen prioridad»), which with «vehículos prioritarios» would be circular; and the literal form keeps
  the answers of iaaa-12075 parallel in length.
- **iaaa-11579/11580/11575** — the «calzada» / «plataforma» / «carril» distinction matches the letters B / C / A of
  the picture (see General decisions).
- **iaaa-5540, iaaa-6691** — "При коя от изброените височини/широчини … :" → «De las siguientes alturas/anchuras,
