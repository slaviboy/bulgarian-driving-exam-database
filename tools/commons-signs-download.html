Bulgarian road-sign SVGs from Wikimedia Commons

One click downloads every SVG of the Commons categories “SVG road signs in Bulgaria” and “Diagrams of road signs of Bulgaria” (route-number and exit plates excluded), plus every SVG shown in the English Wikipedia article “Road signs in Bulgaria”, together with each file’s author and licence. Your browser makes the requests, one per second, like a normal visit to Wikipedia; it takes about 6–8 minutes.

The drawings are CC BY-SA licensed: whoever uses them must credit the authors (the catalogue records them).
